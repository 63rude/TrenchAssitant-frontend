Tokenomics 🪙

The Trench Assistant token ($TRASS) is designed not only to unlock access to the platform, but also to actively shape its evolution through utility and governance.

Token Supply & Allocation 📊

Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 $TRASS

  • Team Allocation: 20% — locked and vested over time to ensure long-term commitment
  • Community Treasury: 5% — a reserve dedicated to rewarding user contributions and incentivizing platform growth
  • Liquidity & Public Sale: 75%

Utility & Access 🔑

Holding $TRASS unlocks access to the Trench Assistant terminal. Starting from Phase 1, a balance of at least 50,000 $TRASS in a wallet is required to use the platform, including personalized multivariate analysis and advanced wallet scanning features.

Governance & Feature Proposals 🗳️

From Phase 2 onward, users who meet the access threshold will be able to submit feature proposals directly through the official website. In Phase 3, governance will expand to include voting mechanisms: token holders will be able to vote on proposed features that they would like to be developed, with voting power proportional to the amount of $TRASS held.

Incentives & Burn Mechanics 🔥

Contributors whose feature proposals receive strong community support through voting will be rewarded with a $TRASS airdrop from the Community Treasury. To help maintain price stability and prevent excessive sell pressure, a proportional amount of tokens will be burned from the team’s allocation for each airdrop distributed. This dual mechanism ensures contributors are rewarded while preserving long-term token value.

The $TRASS token is more than just an access key — it’s a tool for participation, governance, and aligned incentives within a platform built for real traders.
