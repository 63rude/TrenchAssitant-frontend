How It Works

“Easily analyze your smart contract or wallet — just enter an address, and let the assistant do the rest.”

1

Enter Wallet

Paste any Solana wallet address to begin. No connection, no login — just type and submit to start the analysis.

2

Live Analysis

Trench Assistant scans your trading history, pulls token data, and computes personalized stats. This may take a few minutes depending on activity.

3

Get Results

Once the analysis is complete, view win rate, profit per token, market cap correlation, and behavior-based insights — all tailored to your trades.

4

(Coming Soon) Export & Track

Soon you'll be able to download full reports and track your evolving stats over time — with the ability to re-run or compare sessions.
